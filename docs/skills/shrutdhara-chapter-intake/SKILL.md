# ShrutDhara chapter intake

Ship the smallest requested chapter increment with the fidelity of शस्त्रपरिज्ञा or better. Optimize time to a trustworthy first section and total cost per accepted audio minute. Use the project checkout for source and assets. Read [the chapter checklist](references/chapter.md) when matching sources, drafting content, or integrating a release. Read [the audio procedure](references/audio.md) for rendering or comparing voices.

1. **Lock the source boundary.** Identify the Agama, book, study, first and last references, ordered sections, and chosen edition. Resolve conflicting chapter names against printed contents and the next chapter boundary. Search for a rights-cleared original and Hindi edition first, then use a documented historical edition and a separately authored Hindi rendering if needed. Check original, modern translation, digital transcription, source recording, voice weights, and training lineage separately. A free-to-read scan, repository badge, and YouTube listing do not establish redistribution rights. Record what each source permits and keep disputed material outside public assets.
2. **Draft text and speech from one coverage map.** Map every numbered source unit to original text, Hindi meaning, separate explanation, and narration coverage as applicable. Keep edition numbering and stable IDs. Write spoken Hindi as a connected explanation, with transitions that follow the original order. Preserve claims, lists, analogies, and qualifications. Consolidate repeated formulas only when their meaning and scope remain clear. Never invent dialogue or events for a discursive chapter. Treat a derived Hindi rendering as a draft and identify any intermediary translation. When transcription or doctrine is uncertain, flag the exact reference. A complete coverage map is evidence of mapping, not proof of fidelity or editorial review.
3. **Audition before rendering.** Reuse a voice already accepted for the same language, publishing rights, and tone. Otherwise compare short clips of the same real passage, including difficult terms. Prefer local synthesis that meets the baseline. Select on pronunciation, fidelity, and natural spoken rhythm after listening, not on vendor claims. Record voice/version, sample result, estimated cost and time, and the cheapest fallback. Use paid synthesis only within the user's authorized cost and tool terms. For the first usable increment, render a source-bounded section while remaining editorial work proceeds. Label partial coverage and synthetic speech explicitly.
4. **Render by section and review.** Use the cached renderer in [the audio procedure](references/audio.md) or reuse an equivalent existing pipeline. Fix the smallest bad section instead of rendering the whole chapter again. Check playback, omissions or repetitions, all important names and terms, section transitions, and the last sentence. Record what was listened to and who checked it. Keep transcript, audio revision, duration, hashes, and credits aligned. Artifact checks do not establish that the speech is natural or accurate.
5. **Integrate and verify only the requested scope.** For narration-only review, use the existing ShrutDhara Listen review pattern with clear draft status, transcript and credits. For a reviewed chapter, connect the verified assets to the intended journeys and disclose missing components. Preserve old IDs and personal progress. Use distinct media URLs for audio revisions. Deploy only when requested. Smoke-test the exact build and the hosted audio, text, route and seek behavior after deployment. Give the usable result first, then state which quality, rights, or device checks remain open.

When a missing choice blocks publication, finish an independent artifact such as the source map, review script, or local sample. Ask for the one decision or input that unblocks the next increment. Reuse the existing analysis and validation records instead of starting a dossier for each chapter.
