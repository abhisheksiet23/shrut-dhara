ShrutDhara — आचारांग सूत्र, Hindi chapter introductions, version 1

The chapter introductions and synthetic Hindi recordings are review material.
They are concise explanations, not the original sutras or complete translations.
No modern translation or recording is bundled. Modern editions are linked for
reading at the publisher's library and remain subject to their own terms.

Printed page references: Hermann Jacobi, Gaina Sûtras, Part I, Sacred Books
of the East 22, Clarendon Press (1884), Ācārāṅga Sūtra, pp. 1–213.
https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Index:Sacred_Books_of_the_East_-_Volume_22.djvu
https://archive.org/details/gainastras01aco

Hindi chapter names and traditional grouping were checked against the published
Sthānakavāsī editions available at Jain eLibrary, catalogue 003469 and 003437.
The traditional first-book numbering skips the lost seventh study, Mahāparijñā.
Jacobi numbers the eight surviving first-book lectures consecutively.
